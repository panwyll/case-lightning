# Eventualities at every junction: catalogue and build plan

These six catalogues list what can realistically happen at each junction of the flow, with sources. For each one they record what the engine should do (the signal, the issue and what it holds, the task or wait, who is told, how the case recovers) and what it does today.

| Catalogue | Covers | Rows | Built today |
|---|---|---|---|
| [parties.md](parties.md) | Who the clients are and how that changes: a co-buyer leaving, divorce, death, capacity, attorneys, insolvency, sanctions, conflicts, fraud | 77 | ~13% |
| [money.md](money.md) | Source of funds (business owners, overseas, loans, gifts), mortgage changes, ISAs, completion money, refunds | 77 | ~30% |
| [property.md](property.md) | Title, unregistered land, planning and building regulations, searches, surveys, leasehold, the Building Safety Act, new build, auctions, tenanted property | 81 | ~35% |
| [tax.md](tax.md) | SDLT edge by edge (second homes, commercial holdings, homes abroad, replacing a main residence, companies, non-residents, transfers of equity), Wales LTT, seller CGT | 72 | ~15% |
| [exchange.md](exchange.md) | The contract, the deposit, the exchange formulae, authority, chains, notice to complete, failure to complete | 54 | ~22% |
| [completion.md](completion.md) | Undertakings, charges, the day itself, delays, money, registration, discharge, file closure | 99 | ~25% |

## Build plan

The catalogues overlap, so the work is built **by theme**: one mechanism handles many rows. Each theme ships with tests, and the stall detector must stay green throughout.

| # | Theme | Rows it covers | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Consequences: a fact changes, what rested on it is done again.** Changing who the clients are reopens the contract parties, the lender, the funds, the SDLT basis and how they own it. A price or funding change lapses the client's authority to exchange, and they are asked again. Moving the completion date carries the statement, the lender, the advance already received and the linked case with it. A change after exchange holds completion until the seller and lender agree. | parties 1–4, money 2, exchange 3 and 8, tax 3 | **Built** (machine.ts "consequences", tests/unit/engine/consequences.test.ts) |
| B | **Money reconciled, not just recorded.** Every receipt is checked against the completion statement and the deposit. Shortfalls and overpayments become issues. Funds are marked cleared or not, a payment is marked sent, and there is a client ledger for refunds, residual balances and interest. | money 1, 8, 9; completion 5, 9 | **Built** (engine/money.ts; tests/unit/engine/money.test.ts). Still open: interest on client money, the payment-sent record with CHAPS reference, late-completion compensation |
| C | **Readings become typed issues.** Each title, lease, search and contract flag becomes its own issue kind with the right gate and recovery (restrictive covenant, short lease, adverse search, deposit under 10%). This follows the pattern of the TA6 reader. | property 1, 4, 5; exchange 5 | **Built** (engine/findings.ts; tests/unit/engine/findings.test.ts): the register, the lease, the searches and the contract. Still open: the class of title and notices fields in the title reader, a conditional contract's long-stop timer |
| D | **Charges and undertakings.** Charges are a list, so a sale can redeem several, with negative equity checked. TA13 replies and the undertaking to redeem are recorded, and the seller's DS1 is chased after completion. | completion 3, 4 | **Built** (engine/charges.ts; tests/unit/engine/charges.test.ts): other charges as a list, negative equity, our undertaking on a sale and its discharge, the seller's TA13 undertaking on a purchase. Still open: the daily-interest recompute when completion moves, a Help to Buy equity loan redemption on a sale |
| E | **Dates and clocks.** A completion date is refused on a weekend or bank holiday, or after the offer expires. Searches are aged at completion from the date on the search. Clocks for first registration (2 months), the LISA 90-day window, auction (20 working days) and new-build long-stop dates. | property 2, 3, 8; exchange 4; completion 6 | **Built** (engine/dates.ts; tests/unit/engine/dates.test.ts): completion dates refused on a weekend, bank holiday, in the past or after the offer; searches aged at completion from their own date; clocks for first registration, the LISA 90 days, auction completion and the new-build long-stop (recorded from the contract as a task) |
| F | **SDLT per buyer.** The questionnaire at instruction, asked again when facts change. The basis is worked out from the facts, not declared. Wales LTT; the replacing-a-main-residence exception from the client's own chain; a refund diary; the transfer-of-equity charge on debt taken on; a seller CGT flag. | tax 1–10 | **Built** (engine/sdlt-facts.ts, sdlt.ts LTT; tests/unit/engine/sdlt-facts.test.ts): the buyers' answers recorded as a task before exchange and the basis worked out from them with reasons; a changed estimate raised with both figures; Wales as LTT (30-day return to the WRA); the replacement exception (including the client's linked sale) and a three-year refund reminder when the higher rates were paid; contradictions with LISA, Help to Buy ISA and buy-to-let; a transfer of equity's debt taken on counted; the seller's two CGT questions as a flag. Earlier: 19% non-UK company, company relief, £40k floor. Still open: the questionnaire on the client portal, per-buyer answers, the non-resident refund diary |
| G | **People events.** Death, loss of capacity, insolvency, a conflict between joint clients, a SAR/DAML hold with no tipping off, sanctions as a hard stop, the seller identity check and Dreamvar red flags. | parties 2, 3, 4, 5 | **Built** (engine/people.ts; tests/unit/engine/people.test.ts): death, loss of capacity and bankruptcy before and after exchange, our client or the other side, with the lender told; messages to a client who has died stopped at the port; the SAR / DAML hold with deemed consent and the moratorium, no tipping off; sanctions as a hard stop; the seller ID crosscheck. New abandon reasons: client died, capacity, AML, fraud suspected. Still open: a joint-client conflict issue from a notes reading, Dreamvar red-flag scoring, screening the other side |
| H | **After completion.** A person can raise an issue after completion. Land Registry cancellation and extension, the register checked at registration, the title document to the client and lender, stricter file closure. | completion 2, 8, 10 | **Built** (machine.ts "After completion"; tests/unit/engine/after-completion.test.ts): issues after completion; requisition dates and extensions; cancellation; the register check; the seller's DS1 wait on a purchase; stricter closure; retention dates. Still open: the TID attachment to the client, lender retentions, the final bill |
| I | **Co-owners' money on the case.** Contributions per buyer from the proof of funds, the declaration of trust model and its figures, a warning for unequal contributions held as joint tenants. | money 10; parties | **Built** (engine/co-owners.ts; tests/unit/engine/co-owners.test.ts): contributions and the model recorded as a task when tenants in common unequally; shares at purchase fill the declaration's form; unequal money as joint tenants raised for advice. Still open: contributions read from the proof-of-funds sources, a gift to one buyer counted as theirs |

The lender order (the certificate of title only when it can be given unqualified, the advance only against it, an OS1 on every purchase, remortgage checks) was built before this plan.
