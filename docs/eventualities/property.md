# Eventualities: the property (title, searches, survey, leasehold, property forms)

What a competent England & Wales conveyancer plans for at each junction where the *property itself* can derail a transaction, checked against the engine as it stands on 2026-10-01. The companion registers are `docs/engine-eventualities.md` (the spine) and `docs/conditions-register.md` (conditions per sub-flow); this one is the property backlog.

**Where the engine is today, in one paragraph.** Documents are read well: title register, title plan, lease, management pack, searches, TA6/TA7, survey and specialist reports each have an extractor (`extraction.ts`). The weak part is what follows. Title, lease and search findings become **flags on one generic decision** (`title_flagged` / `search_flagged`, options approve / refer / request further / indemnity / escalate). Nothing turns a specific finding into a **typed issue with a gate, an owner and a recovery path**. Of the property issue kinds in `issues.ts:304-371`, `restrictive_covenant`, `title_restriction`, `search_adverse_entry`, `short_lease`, `ground_rent_issue`, `lease_defect`, `valuation_issue` and `third_party_consent` are never raised automatically; only a person raises them (`raise_issue`). The TA6/TA7 reader (`property-forms.ts:29-47`) is the exception: each material answer is already a typed issue. That reader is the pattern to copy.

**Status key.** **BUILT** = the engine detects the signal and does the correct thing (file:line). **PARTIAL** = detected or flagged, but the response is generic, ungated or incomplete. **MISSING** = a person has to know.
**Priority.** **P1** = can cause a void or defective title, a lender loss, an uninsurable or unmortgageable property, or an exchange the client was not advised on. **P2** = causes delay or rework, or a professional-conduct gap. **P3** = refinement.

Sources are abbreviated as follows. **Handbook** = UK Finance Mortgage Lenders' Handbook Part 1 (cited by topic, because the text is behind a login and its paragraph numbers move). **PG n** = HM Land Registry practice guide n. **Protocol** = Law Society Conveyancing Protocol. **LRA** = Land Registration Act 2002. **LPA** = Law of Property Act 1925. **LFRA** = Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024. **BSA** = Building Safety Act 2022.
