# Eventualities: contract, exchange and chain

This file lists what can happen between agreeing the draft contract and the contract being performed, or failing to be performed. It covers seven junctions: negotiating the deal, the draft contract, signing, readiness and authority, the exchange itself, exchange to completion, and completion failure, plus the chain that runs across all of them. For each eventuality it gives the signal the engine sees, the correct response, and what the code does today. Written 2026-10-01 against `main` at `a00f458`. No code was changed.

**Engine today**: **BUILT** means the engine handles it end to end. **PARTIAL** means part of the response exists and the gap is named. **MISSING** means nothing exists.
**Priority**: **P1** means money, liability or a binding commitment is at stake and it should be built first. **P2** means real cases hit it regularly. **P3** means rare, or a refinement.

**Sources** (cited by tag)
- **[P19]**: Law Society Conveyancing Protocol (2019), Stages C–E. Step 20: agree the deposit and the completion date; advise the buyer on the consequences of a deposit held to order, below 10% or not held as stakeholder; take account of any reservation deposit. Step 21: the buyer has insurance ready "on exchange". Step 22: exchange by a Law Society formula and tell the client and agent at once.
- **[LSF]**: Law Society formulae for exchanging contracts by telephone. Formula A (1986): one firm holds both parts. Formula B (1986): each firm holds its own client's part. Formula C (1989): chains. Part I is an irrevocable "release" to exchange if called back before a stated time that same day. Part II is the exchange, with the deposit sent or passed up the chain on Formula C terms. After exchange the firm writes a memorandum recording the time, the formula, any agreed variations, the completion date, the deposit and who spoke. https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/property/formulae-for-exchanging-contracts-by-telephone
- **[SCS]**: Standard Conditions of Sale, 5th ed. (2018 revision).
  - Deposit: 2.2 (10% by exchange; a dishonoured deposit lets the seller treat the contract as discharged; 2.2.5 the deposit may be used as a deposit on a related residential purchase in England and Wales; otherwise it is held as stakeholder).
  - Risk and insurance: 5.1 (the seller keeps the physical state and the risk, but has no duty to insure).
  - Occupation by the buyer before completion: 5.2 (a licence with a fee, the buyer pays outgoings and insures, ends on 5 working days' notice).
  - Completion: 6.1 (20 working days by default; money received after 2pm counts as next working day).
  - Notice to complete: 6.8 (10 working days; a buyer who paid less than 10% must top it up to 10% once a notice is served).
  - Remedies: 7.2 (compensation for late completion at the contract rate on the price less the deposit); 7.4 and 7.5 (failure to comply with a notice: rescission, forfeit or return of the deposit, resale and damages).
  - Other: 3.3 (leases affecting the property); 9 (contents).
  - https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/property/standard-conditions-of-sale
- **[CCP]**: Law Society Code for Completion by Post (2019).
- **[UKF]**: UK Finance Mortgage Lenders' Handbook, Part 1 (exchange, insurance, the certificate of title, offer validity) and Part 2 (each lender's own rules, including electronic signatures).
- **[LPMPA]**: Law of Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1989 s.2. The contract, and any variation of a material term (price, completion date), must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- **[LPA49]**: Law of Property Act 1925 s.49(2). The court has discretion to order a deposit repaid.
- **[TDA]**: Trustee Delegation Act 1999 s.7 and the two-trustee rule (LPA 1925 s.27). One attorney cannot give a valid receipt for capital money for two co-owners.
- **[PG82]**: HMLR Practice Guide 82, electronic signatures accepted by HM Land Registry.
- **[Walford]** Walford v Miles [1992] 2 AC 128 and **[Pitt]** Pitt v PHH Asset Management [1994] 1 WLR 327. A lock-out is enforceable only for a definite period and with consideration. An agreement to negotiate is not enforceable.
- **[Race]**: Law Society guidance on contract races. The seller's solicitor tells every prospective buyer's solicitor about the race, with the seller's consent. If the seller refuses consent, the solicitor stops acting. https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/guidance/contract-races
- **[NHQC]**: New Homes Quality Code and Consumer Code for Home Builders. These cover the reservation agreement, the reservation fee and its refund, the exchange deadline and long-stop dates.
- **[Street]** Street v Mountford [1985] AC 809. Exclusive occupation for a rent is a tenancy, whatever the document calls it.
- **[HA04]**: Housing Act 2004 ss.213–214 (protecting tenancy deposits). **[RRA]**: Renters' Rights Act 2025, whose main tenancy reforms start in 2026. Check the commencement status before relying on either.

## Top 10 to build first

1. **Make the exchange authority specific and let it lapse** (4.1, 4.2, 1.2, 1.6).
   - `client_decision_recorded(exchange_authority)` gains `scope: { pricePennies, depositPennies, completionDate | 'to be agreed', conditions? }` and an attendance-note document.
   - `exchangeAuthorityHolds` returns true when the price, deposit, completion date or contract approval about to be exchanged differs from the scope.
   - The projection clears `contractApprovedAt` and the authority on `price_changed`, or when the contract changes after approval.
   - Gate `contracts_exchanged` on `cmd.completionDate` matching the scope.
   - Add a no-stall case: price change after authority leads to the authority being asked again, then exchange.
2. **Validate the completion date at exchange** (4.6, 5.5, 5.6, 2.8).
   - In `contracts_exchanged`, reject a date in the past, or a completion date later than the mortgage offer's expiry when there is no `offer_extended` resolution.
   - Raise a warning issue (gate `none`, attached to the exchange decision) for a weekend or bank holiday (`working-days.ts`), or for fewer than N working days before the lender's funds (a lender-profile field).
   - When completion is the same day, the funds and certificate-of-title checks become exchange-time refusals.
   - Raise `mortgage_offer_expiring` to gate `exchange` when the target completion date is later than the expiry.
3. **Rule-based contract terms** (2.2, 2.3, 2.4).
   - A `contractRules(facts, state)` in `rules.ts` covering: deposit below 10%, deposit held as agent, special conditions excluding SCS 7.2 or 6.8, notice-to-complete days below 10, conditional contracts, and a price that does not match the case.
   - Each raises a cited issue on approval (`deposit_issue`, or a new `contract_term` kind), resolved by `deposit_agreed` or `accepted_as_is` with an `accept_risk` client decision.
4. **Accepted risk tied to an item** (4.3, 4.5).
   - `accept_risk` takes `scope: issueIds | enquiryIds` and an advice document. It resolves those items as `accepted_as_is`, referencing the decision.
   - `withdraw_enquiry` on a purchase requires that decision.
   - Extend `surveyHolds` to "booked, report not back" unless risk is accepted.
5. **Exchange memorandum and formula** (5.1, 5.4, 5.8).
   - Extend the payload: `formula: 'A'|'B'|'C'|'auction'|'in_person'`, `spokeWith`, `depositPennies`, `depositRoute: 'direct'|'up_chain'|'held_by_us'`, and `variations`.
   - Generate a memorandum .docx into the case files and send a confirmation to the client.
   - On the seller side, open a `deposit` wait until the deposit is confirmed received (closed by `deposit_received`).
6. **A completion-date change that carries everything with it** (6.1, 8.2).
   - `change_completion_date` requires a `completion_date: agreed` client decision made after the last change, and a variation document.
   - The service applies the same change to the linked matter in step and refuses if that side cannot change.
   - It tells the lender (a first request), regenerates the completion statement and re-requests a stale redemption figure.
7. **Serving a notice to complete, rescission and deposit outcome** (7.3, 7.5).
   - Due task "Serve notice to complete" when `completion_failure` is open and our side was ready (the funds, documents and checks the engine already knows).
   - Draft the notice from a template. The expiry is calculated as 10 working days excluding the day of service (or the contract's `noticeToCompleteDays`).
   - On expiry, a client decision: rescind or extend.
   - A new terminal command `rescind_contract {by, depositOutcome, claimNote}` emits `matter_rescinded`, which is distinct from `abandoned`. It is allowed only after exchange and after a notice has expired, and it keeps an open "deposit / claim" task until closed.
   - `abandon_matter` is refused after exchange.
8. **Contract-rate compensation and exposure** (7.2, 7.4).
   - Extract `contractRate` from the contract; the Law Society rate is a firm setting.
   - `completion.ts` or a new `compensation.ts` calculates, in whole pennies, the daily figure and the running total from the contractual date. It goes on the completion statement as a line, and into the defaulting client's exposure letter: compensation, the top-up to 10%, forfeit and loss on resale.
