# CONVEYi — AppSource listing: post-install instructions

Everything needed to make the "how do I actually open this?" step obvious on the store
listing. Two deliverables: **screenshots** (below) and **description copy** (below that).

⚠️ **Any listing edit reopens certification.** Metadata-only changes are still a new
submission and go back through review. The app went live 2026-08-04, so batch this with the
£200 pricing copy rather than submitting twice.

---

## Why the store has to carry this

There is **no manifest-driven post-install callout for Outlook add-ins**. The `<GetStarted>`
element that produces that callout is task-pane only — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote — and
is a child of `DesktopFormFactor` in the Task pane 1.0 VersionOverrides schema. This is a
`MailApp` manifest, so it cannot use it.

AppSource's own line, *"This app can … when you're reading a message"*, is generated by
Microsoft from the manifest's permissions and extension point and is **not editable**. It says
*when* the add-in runs, never *how to open it*.

So the store listing and the in-product tour are the only two places this can be fixed.

## 1. Screenshots (the high-leverage part)

Partner Center → **Marketplace listings** → screenshots. Requirements per Microsoft's
marketplace listing docs: **1280 × 720 px, .PNG, minimum 1, maximum 5.** Confirm against the
upload control in Partner Center, which states the current requirement.

Captions matter as much as the images — AppSource renders them under each shot.

### Shot 1 — "Find it under Apps" *(the missing step)*
- Outlook with a realistic client email open (use dummy data — **no real client information**).
- The **Apps** button in the message toolbar highlighted with a coloured ring or arrow.
- The Apps flyout open, CONVEYi visible in the list.
- **Caption:** "Open any client email and choose CONVEYi from the Apps button."

### Shot 2 — "Pin it" *(the step that makes installs look broken)*
- The CONVEYi pane open beside the email.
- The **pin icon** at the top of the pane circled/arrowed, ideally shown mid-click.
- **Caption:** "Pin the pane once, and it stays open as you move from email to email."

### Shot 3 — The pane doing its job
- A matched matter with a drafted reply on screen.
- **Caption:** "CONVEYi reads the thread, matches the matter, and prepares the reply."

### Shot 4 — Draft-only reassurance
- The Drafts folder with a CONVEYi-prepared reply sitting in it.
- **Caption:** "Every reply lands in your Drafts. CONVEYi never sends email for you."

### Shot 5 *(optional)* — the case picture / matter board.

Notes: keep annotations to one colour, matching the violet brand (`#5A27E0`). Real content
rather than empty windows. Strip personal data from every frame.

**These need capturing in real Outlook** — not something that can be generated from the repo.

## 2. Description copy (the text instructions)

Partner Center → **Marketplace listings** → Description. Accepts basic HTML. Put this block
directly under the opening value-proposition paragraph, before the feature list — high enough
that it is visible without expanding "read more".

```html
<p><strong>Getting started takes about a minute.</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Open any client email</strong> in Outlook — on Windows, Mac or the web.</li>
  <li><strong>Click Apps</strong> in the message toolbar (under the <strong>&hellip;</strong>
      more menu in some versions of Outlook) and choose <strong>CONVEYi</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Pin the pane</strong> using the pin icon at the top of it. Outlook opens add-ins
      unpinned, which means the pane closes each time you move to another email until you pin
      it. This is the step most people miss.</li>
  <li><strong>Sign in</strong> with the same Microsoft 365 work account. CONVEYi then follows
      you from email to email, reading the thread and preparing the reply.</li>
</ol>
<p>CONVEYi never sends email on your behalf — every reply is saved to your Drafts for you to
review and send.</p>
```

### Also worth updating: the summary

The summary is what shows in search results (max 100 characters, ~70 recommended). It should
sell the value, not the mechanics — the instructions above cover the mechanics.

## 3. Optional, only if the package is rebuilt anyway

The **Supertip** is the tooltip Outlook shows when someone hovers the button in the Apps menu —
the exact moment they are deciding what this is. Currently, in
`conveyi-appsource-manifest.xml` (and `public/addin/manifest.xml`):

```xml
<bt:String id="TaskpaneButton.Tooltip"
  DefaultValue="Open the case-aware drafting &amp; case-management sidebar."/>
```

That describes rather than instructs. A version that does the pin's work:

```xml
<bt:String id="TaskpaneButton.Tooltip"
  DefaultValue="Opens the CONVEYi pane. Pin it to keep it open as you move between emails."/>
```

Changing this means a **version bump (1.0.5.0 → 1.0.6.0) and a new package upload** on top of
the listing edit — worth doing only if the package is being rebuilt for another reason.
`SupportsPinning` is already `true` in both manifests, so the pin genuinely exists.
